## App Description (English)
**Pomo Time** is a professional Pomodoro timer app designed for effective time management and productivity enhancement.

### Key Features:
🍅 **Classic Pomodoro Technique** - 25 min work + 5 min break cycles
⏰ **Customizable Timers** - Set your preferred work and break durations
📊 **Analytics & Statistics** - Daily, weekly, and monthly productivity reports
🎯 **Task Management** - Plan and track your work efficiently
🔥 **Motivation System** - Streaks, deep work sessions, and scoring
🎵 **Background Sounds** - Rain, ocean, birds, and more ambient sounds
📱 **Offline Functionality** - Works completely without internet
🌙 **Dark Mode** - Eye-friendly interface for any time of day
🔔 **Smart Notifications** - Get notified when sessions complete
📈 **Productivity Insights** - Track your efficiency over time

### Why Choose Pomo Time?
- Clean and intuitive interface
- Science-backed Pomodoro technique
- Extensive customization options
- Completely free with no ads
- Privacy-focused design
